The 3 A.M.
Shift

You work alone in the convenience store of a gas station up in the hills of Brazil, eight nights in a row. It's raining. The radio stays on all night, counting down the shows left before it goes off the air. Every customer has a story, and what you do changes how it all ends. Every night, something is slightly out of place. It might just be the tiredness.

  • At the till: serve whoever comes in, make coffee, sweep, restock the shelves.
  • In the dining room: heat up the snacks, wipe the tables, refill the machine and pick the TV channel. Anyone who buys food sits down to eat.
  • Drink coffee or grab something from the machine to stay awake. When you're sleepy, you can't trust what you see.
  • Talk, help or ignore: your choices carry across all eight nights, and there are six endings.
  • What you find out goes into the notebook. It keeps the clues and endings from every time you play.
